# Next increment ideas

- **Top priority.** `hit()` and `stand()` are called from the render buttons (`this.hit()`, `this.stand()`) but neither method exists anywhere in `App.js` — the game still can't be played past placing a bet. Note this is now *reachable*: the Hit/Stand buttons are gated on `disabled={!currentBet || gameOver}`, and before the bet-input fix `currentBet` could never be set, so they were permanently disabled and the missing methods were harmless. Now that a bet can actually be placed the buttons enable, and tapping either throws `this.hit is not a function`. Implementing them is the natural next small fix.
- `startNewGame` reads `this.state.deck`/`this.state.wallet` into local `let` bindings but never reassigns them into state consistently on the `"continue"` branch when neither condition is met (deck/wallet unchanged is fine, but the branch structure is easy to misread) — worth a close look once `hit`/`stand` exist and mutate deck/count.
- No dealer-turn or win/lose logic exists yet (no bust check, no blackjack check, no comparison of final counts) — will be needed once hit/stand are implemented.
- `getCardImage` builds a relative `./assets/cards/...png` URI; worth confirming these assets actually exist under `assets/cards/` before wiring up `hit`/`stand`, since a missing-asset bug would look like a silent rendering failure similar to the one just fixed.
